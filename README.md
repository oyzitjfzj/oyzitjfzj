## About

I build and explore **AI systems, autonomous agents, and systems software** with a strong focus on capability, efficiency, reliability, and portability.

My work is driven by a simple question:

> **How far can intelligent systems be pushed without accepting unnecessary complexity or waste?**

Most active projects are currently developed in **private repositories** while they are under active research and engineering.

## Current Focus

- 🧠 **Efficient Intelligence** — compact, resource-aware AI architectures and learning systems
- 🤖 **Autonomous Systems** — agents that can reason, act, use tools, and adapt
- ⚙️ **Systems Engineering** — reliable foundations, protocols, runtimes, and infrastructure
- 🔬 **Research Engineering** — turning ambitious ideas into testable architectures and working systems

## Engineering Principles

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **First principles** | Understand the system before copying the pattern. |
| **Capability + efficiency** | Strong systems should use resources intelligently. |
| **Reliability** | Architecture matters as much as raw capability. |
| **Portability** | Good systems should adapt across different hardware and environments. |
| **Evidence over hype** | Ideas become valuable when they can be tested. |
